# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

---

## Overview
> "Friends don't lie." — **Eleven**

*Stranger Things* is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers. It is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana.

---

## Key Information
* **Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
* **Release Year:** 2016
* **Creators:** The Duffer Brothers

---

## Main Characters
1. **Eleven** (played by Millie Bobby Brown)
2. **Mike Wheeler** (played by Finn Wolfhard)
3. **Dustin Henderson** (played by Gaten Matarazzo)
4. **Lucas Sinclair** (played by Caleb McLaughlin)
5. **Will Byers** (played by Noah Schnapp)

---

## Why I Like This Series
* *Nostalgic 80s Vibe:* The music, clothing, and references are very well done.
* *Compelling Plot:* The mystery behind the **Upside Down** keeps you on the edge of your seat.
* *Great Character Development:* Each character grows significantly across seasons.
